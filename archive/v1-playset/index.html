How to run it

  • DraftShuffle the cube and deal 15-card packs. Pick one card, pass the rest. Pass left, then right, then left.
  • DeckBuild from your picks plus any number of Basic Energy. There is no limit on copies of a card.
  • RulesUse the current rules. The player who goes first cannot attack or play a Supporter on their first turn.
  • Old textPoké-Powers and Poké-Bodies count as Abilities. This affects Claydol, Porygon2 and Sableye.
  • Rule BoxThe cube has no ex, V, GX, EX, Prism Star, Radiant or ACE SPEC cards. Text that mentions them does nothing, so Twin Energy always gives two Colorless Energy.

Balance notes

How the types stay level

  • Every type has the same 21-card shape, at least one engine Pokémon for draw, search or Energy, and a main attacker that does 120 to 240 damage.
  • The biggest attacks have a cost. Charizard, Raichu, Zapdos and Arcanine discard Energy. Cobalion, Copperajah and Obstagoon's Knuckle Impact skip a turn. Machamp's Dynamite Punch damages itself.
  • Most Stage 2 Pokémon have 150 to 180 HP, so a finished line survives one hit from most attackers.
  • Psychic hits for the least damage (120), but it has the best draw engine, Dusclops's 50 damage anywhere, and Weakness on every Fighting attacker.
  • Each type has one matching Special Energy. The generic Special Energy helps decks that use two types.
  • Colorless engines (Pidgeotto, Bibarel and Porygon2) fit any deck, so expect them to go early.

Watch these in the first few drafts

  • Fire speed. Stoke, Charizard's Burn Brightly and Arcanine's 240 can end games quickly. If it is too fast, replace the 240-damage Arcanine with a smaller one.
  • Palafin. Valiant Evolution into a 210-damage Justice Kick is the strongest two-Energy play in the cube.
  • Damage counters on the Bench. Dusclops, Obstagoon, Absol and Hawlucha can pick off small support Pokémon such as Pidgeotto and Kirlia. Machoke and Shaymin protect the Bench, so keep both in the cube.
  • Fire has Weakness coverage on both Grass and Metal, the widest in the cube. Weakness Policy and two Coating Metal Energy are in the cube as answers. If Fire still wins too often, this is the first place to look.
  • Sudowoodo's Roadblock limits the opponent to four Benched Pokémon. This is mild with 40-card decks, but cut it if your group dislikes it.
