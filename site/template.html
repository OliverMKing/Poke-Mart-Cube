Poké Mart Cube

A cross-era singleton cube built from cards you can buy for pocket money. There is one copy of every card and one card for every Pokémon species, so each evolution line is a single Basic, Stage 1 and Stage 2. Every Pokémon gives up one Prize card, and every type has the same shape.

How to run it

  • DraftShuffle the cube and deal each player four 15-card packs. Pick one card, pass the rest. Change the passing direction for each pack.
  • DeckBuild a 50-card deck from your 60 picks plus any number of Basic Energy. Play with 5 Prize cards. Most decks run three or four short evolution lines plus several Basics.
  • SingletonEach card is in the cube once, and each Pokémon species appears on one card only. Draft the Stage 2 first, because nobody else needs your Basic.
  • RulesUse the current rules. The player who goes first cannot attack or play a Supporter on their first turn.
  • Old textPoké-Powers and Poké-Bodies count as Abilities. This affects Claydol, Donphan, Porygon2, Sableye and Unown Q. Old cards that just say Trainer, such as Pokémon Breeder, count as Items.
  • Rule BoxThe cube has no ex, V, GX, EX, Prism Star, Radiant or ACE SPEC cards. Text that mentions them does nothing, so Twin Energy always gives two Colorless Energy.

The shape of the cube

Every type has two Stage 2 lines (1-1-1), four Stage 1 lines (1-1) and seven Basics. Colorless has one more Basic. The Basic-heavy mix follows Chaos Cube, the most-liked singleton cube on CubeKoga.

Balance notes

How the types stay level

  • Every type has the same 21-card shape: two Stage 2 lines, four Stage 1 lines and seven Basics. Each type has at least one draw or search engine and at least one way to accelerate Energy.
  • The biggest attacks have a cost. Charizard, Cinderace, Raichu, Zapdos and Arcanine discard Energy. Breloom, Yanmega, Cobalion and Electivire cannot attack on the next turn. Machamp, Rapidash and Gyarados damage themselves.
  • Most Stage 2 Pokémon have 150 to 180 HP, so a finished line survives one hit from most attackers.
  • Darkness and Psychic top out at 160 fixed damage, the lowest in the cube. Darkness makes up for it with attacks that grow during the game (Zoroark, Spiritomb and Galarian Moltres) and with damage counters.
  • Psychic's Kirlia can become Gardevoir for draw power or Gallade, which hits for 160. Mewtwo hits for 180 late in the game, and Mesprit hits for 160 when Uxie and Azelf are on your Bench.
  • Seventeen generic Special Energy cards and Crispin help decks that use two types.

Finishing a line in singleton

  • Take the Stage 2 first. With six drafters, each 15-card pack comes back to you twice, at picks 7 and 13. The Basic and Stage 1 from that pack often wheel, because nobody else needs them.
  • Rare Candy, Pokémon Breeder, Wally and Evosoda help finish a Stage 2 line. Evolution Incense, Timer Ball, Capturing Aroma, Jacq and Hilda find the missing piece.
  • A 1-1 Stage 1 line plus strong Basics is a complete plan. Every type has seven Basics, and several of them attack well on their own.

Watch these in the first few drafts

  • Fire speed. Stoke, Charizard's Burn Brightly, Chi-Yu and Arcanine's 240 can end games quickly. Five-prize games give slower decks more time to answer.
  • Weakness coverage. Fighting and Fire each hit about 40 Pokémon in the cube for double damage, twice as many as most types. Weakness Policy and Coating Metal Energy are the answers in the cube.
  • Damage counters on the Bench. Dusclops, Dusknoir, Obstagoon, Absol and Hawlucha can pick off small support Pokémon. Machoke stops both damage and damage counters on your Bench. Shaymin and Manaphy stop attack damage to the Bench.
  • Cofagrigus. Its Law of the Underworld puts 6 damage counters on every Pokémon with an Ability, on both sides, for one Energy. It wrecks Ability engines, so watch how often it decides games.
  • Hex Maniac turns off every Ability for a turn. This cube has many Ability engines, so it is strong. Cut it if it feels unfair.
  • Darkness damage. Its best fixed attack is Mightyena's 160, which also damages Mightyena. If Darkness decks lose often, swap Galarian Obstagoon for the Champion's Path version, which hits for 180.
  • Sudowoodo and Collapsed Stadium both limit the Bench to four. Decks with four lines feel this more, so cut them if your group dislikes it.

If you play 8 players with 4 prizes

Some cards get a bonus while a player has 4 or fewer Prize cards left. In 4-prize games that bonus is on from the first turn, so Kartana, Gouging Fire, Lacey and Roxanne are left out. With 5 prizes the bonus starts only after the first knockout, so you can add them back.
